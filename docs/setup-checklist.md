# Setup checklist (for a real local business)

1. Confirm they have a Google Business Profile.
2. In GBP: **Get more reviews** → copy the review link (looks like `https://g.page/r/.../review`).
3. Paste the link into `templates/customer-message.txt`.
4. Have the owner approve the wording (must sound like them).
5. Decide the trigger:
   - **Manual:** text the template when the job is done.
   - **Later:** wire to their scheduler/CRM or a simple automation tool.
6. Self-test: send the message to yourself; tap the link; confirm the review form opens.
7. Happy path → Google. Unhappy path → owner replies privately (do not push unhappy customers to leave a public review).

## How to spot a good first client

- Been open a while, still low review count vs peers
- Recent happy 5-star reviews exist
- Owner-operated / field crew (no office person chasing reviews)
